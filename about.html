About Crazy Chicken

Road Survival Arcade Game

Welcome to Nabil's Crazy Chicken

Crazy Chicken is a browser-based arcade survival game created by Nabil Mahmud. The game challenges players to guide a chicken across dangerous roads, rivers, and other obstacles while trying to reach the finish line.

The idea behind the game is to create a simple experience that anyone can pick up and play, while still offering challenges, progression, rewards, and reasons to improve your score.

Why This Game Was Created

Crazy Chicken is an independent project built out of creativity, curiosity, and a passion for making games. It is a personal project created to turn an idea into a playable experience that people can enjoy on their phones and computers.

This project is also part of an ongoing journey to learn, experiment with game development, and create more digital experiences in the future.

Game Features

  • 20 levels of road survival gameplay
  • Cars, trucks, rivers, logs, and train obstacles
  • Coin collection and score tracking
  • Unlockable chicken skins
  • Sound effects and simple controls
  • Mobile and computer gameplay
  • Offline play support after loading and caching

Our Goal

Our goal is to keep creating simple, enjoyable, and accessible games. We hope Crazy Chicken gives players a fun way to spend a few minutes, challenge themselves, beat their best scores, and enjoy each new level.
