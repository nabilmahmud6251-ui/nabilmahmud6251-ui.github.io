🐔 Crazy Chicken Game Guide

Learn how to survive the road, collect coins, and reach the finish!

Welcome to Crazy Chicken

Welcome to Nabil's Crazy Chicken, a free road-survival arcade game where your goal is to guide a chicken through dangerous environments and reach the finish line.

Watch out for traffic, rivers, trains, and other dangers. Every move matters. Try to survive, collect coins, improve your score, and progress through 20 levels.

▶ Play Crazy Chicken

How to Play

  • Move your chicken forward to get closer to the finish line.
  • Avoid cars and trucks on the road.
  • Cross rivers by moving onto floating logs.
  • Be careful around train tracks.
  • Collect coins whenever you can safely reach them.
  • Keep moving so you don't get caught by the eagle.
  • Reach the end of the level to unlock your next challenge.

Game Controls

Choose the controls that work best for your device.

📱 Mobile

Tap the screen to move forward one step.

Swipe left or right to move sideways.

Swipe down to move backward.

💻 Computer

Use the arrow keys to move.

You can also use W, A, S, D.

Spacebar or the Up Arrow moves forward.

Know Your Obstacles

🚗 Cars

Cars travel across the road. Watch their movement and find a safe moment to cross.

🚚 Trucks

Trucks are another road hazard. Stay alert and avoid stepping into their path.

🌊 Rivers and Logs

Rivers are dangerous to cross on foot. Use the floating logs to move across the water and avoid falling in.

🚆 Trains

Train tracks can become dangerous when a train passes. Pay attention to warning signals and wait for a safe opportunity.

🦅 Eagle

Don't stay still for too long. Keep making progress to avoid the eagle that can end your run.

Coins and Chicken Skins

Coins can be collected during gameplay. They add to your coin balance and can be used to unlock different chicken skins from the game's skin menu.

Available skins include:

  • Classic – your starting chicken.
  • Golden – a gold-colored chicken.
  • Ninja – a ninja-inspired design.
  • Pinky – a pink chicken.
  • Rainbow – a colorful chicken.

Choose your favorite skin and keep playing to collect enough coins for the others.

Levels and Progress

Crazy Chicken includes 20 levels. Each level challenges you to survive and reach the finish while avoiding hazards. Your score, best score, coins, and level progress are designed to be saved on your device.

As you advance, the game becomes more challenging. Stay alert, make careful moves, and try to beat your previous best score.

Survival Tips

  • Don't rush into traffic without checking the movement of vehicles.
  • Plan your route before stepping onto a river log.
  • Pay attention to train warning lights and sounds.
  • Collect coins only when the path is safe.
  • Keep moving and avoid staying in one place for too long.
  • Use small, careful movements when you're surrounded by hazards.
Tip: Surviving is more important than collecting every coin. A safe route is often the best route.

About the Game

Nabil's Crazy Chicken is an independent arcade-style survival game created by Nabil Mahmud. It was made to offer a simple, fun, pick-up-and-play experience with quick controls, colorful graphics, challenging obstacles, and unlockable rewards.

The game can be played in a mobile browser and is designed to support offline play after the game has been loaded and its offline features are available.
